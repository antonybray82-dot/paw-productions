Welcome to Planet Marshmallow!

Catch falling purple, pink and white marshmallows! Stack three or more touching marshmallows of the same colour to make them disappear. Create chain reactions for bonus points! Watch for a rare 🐾 Amos rescue token to clear the highest occupied row!

Use ◀ ▶ and DROP, or drag and tap the game board.

Match 3+ colours • 💎 Crystal bonus • 🐾 Amos clears the highest row!

How to play: Guide each falling marshmallow into a column. Match three or more of the same colour horizontally or vertically. Marshmallows above a match tumble down. Keep the stack below the top!
Keyboard: ← → to steer • ↓ or Space to drop • P to pause
